About Me

Bianca Lee.

I'm a Front-End Developer with over 20 years of experience in design, bringing a creative eye and a thoughtful approach to building digital experiences.

My career began in graphic design and evolved into web design and development, giving me a strong foundation in visual storytelling, user experience, and responsive design. From crafting digital layouts to hand-coding HTML and CSS email campaigns, I've developed an appreciation for both the creative and technical sides of the web.

Today, I'm continuing to strengthen my front-end development skills, with a focus on creating responsive, accessible, and visually engaging websites. I bring a unique combination of design experience, attention to detail, and a genuine passion for turning creative ideas into functional digital experiences.

Beautiful design. Thoughtful code. A distinctive identity.
